# Code of Conduct

We want Registration Enhancer to be welcoming, respectful, and constructive.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and considerate.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Share reproducible information and assume good faith.
- Respect privacy; never publish secrets or personal data.
- Accept constructive feedback.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove unacceptable content and restrict participation when necessary. Report concerns privately through the contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile.
